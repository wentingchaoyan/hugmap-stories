## Key Assumptions & Risks

### Growth Assumptions
| KPI | Assumption | Rationale | Risk |
|-----|-----------|-----------|------|
| **DAU/MAU Ratio** | 30% | Child dev apps typically 25-35% (high engagement) | Could be 15-20% if adoption slow |
| **Monthly Churn** | 3-4% | Parent loyalty high, but some attrition as child ages | Could be 5-7% if product-market fit weak |
| **LTV (48 months)** | ¥72k (Japan) | Assumes 4-year engagement window (child ages 0-4) | Could drop if older-child segments low |
| **Series A Conversion** | 25-30% | Typical parenting app free-to-paid for quality products | Could be 10-15% if messaging weak |

### Market Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| **Competitive entry** (Google Family Link, Apple features) | IP + character moat + Network effects (SNS sharing) |
| **Regulatory** (child data privacy, GDPR/CCPA strict) | Privacy-first design, on-device processing, clear consent flows |
| **Unit economics** (CAC too high in competitive markets) | Organic + organic growth focused, strong NPS referral |
| **Global localization** (culture-specific needs differ) | Community-driven content, local partnership model |

## Competitive Advantages (Moat)

### 1. Character IP & Emotional Connection
- Parents invest emotionally → high stickiness
- IP standalone revenue stream (非競争領域)
- 他のアプリは真似できない「世界観」

### 2. Participatory Growth Frame = User-Generated Content Loop
- 親が子どものsnapshots SNS投稿 → viral growth（organic）
- Competitor との差別化：記録が「物語」に変わる体験

### 3. Data Defensibility
- 毎月300回以上の記録 (lock-in強い)
- 成長記録は「家族の思い出」 = switch不可能に近い

### 4. Professional Trust Built Bottom-Up
- toB営業なし → 親が勝手に園・学校に紹介
- Support of professionals without sales cost

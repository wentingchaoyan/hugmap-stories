Explore three moments with Mimo
SCENE 01

First, notice the child’s sound

Without correcting the sound, notice who the child looks toward and what happens next.

SCENE 02

Then, offer one small response

Return one similar sound or a smile. Keep the response brief.

SCENE 03

And then, leave a little space

Do not prompt the next turn right away. A look or small smile can also be a response.

A quick note from Mimo

Look for openings, not a score

  • Notice where the child’s sound, gaze, or expression is directed
  • Offer one brief response
  • Leave time for the child to begin the next turn

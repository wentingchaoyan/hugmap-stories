HUGMAP · MEET THE BUDDIES

Hello,
I’m Mimo.

I’d love to hear
what you have to say.

Open the book ↓

01

I love a good song.

And a good chat, too.
But do you know what I love most?

Listening to you.

↓

02

You say, “Ahh!”

“Ahh!” I say back.
Then you say, “Ooh!”
“Ooh!” I say.

We’re having a little chat
of our own.

↓

03

Up go your arms.
Up go my wings!

I can do that too!
Shall we do it again?

There we go—together!

↓

04

What have you spotted?

You point, and I look.
Over there?

Oh, now I see it!
Thanks for showing me.

↓

05

Which would you like?

The book or the ball?
You point to the book.

A story it is!
Let’s read it together.

↓

06

Ooh, what’s on this page?

You point to something in the book.
I lean in for a closer look.

Now you’ve got me curious!

↓

07

You have a story, too.

You show me a photo.
I want to know all about it.
“And then?” I ask. “What happened next?”

Oops, you weren’t finished.
Take your time. I’m listening.

↓

08

Shall we read some more?

I settle into my chair.
And wait.
And wait.

Hmm. The book’s still closed.
You’re waiting too…

↓

09

Oh! You’re waiting
for me!

There I was, waiting for you,
when it was my turn all along.

I open the book and begin.
“Once upon a time…”

↓

10

Thanks for listening!

A sound, a look, a little wave…
You don’t have to use words.

Whatever you want to share,
I’m here to listen.

↓

A note for grown-ups

Little moments.
So much to share.

Mimo loves being with children: copying their sounds, following a pointing finger and sharing a laugh. She is curious about what each child has to say, whether they use words, sounds or gestures.

If you’re wondering when your child will start talking, the wait can feel long. It can help to notice the conversations already happening: a glance your way, a finger pointing something out, a laugh you share. Those moments are worth enjoying in their own right.

Mimo can be so relaxed that she forgets it’s her turn. But beneath that easygoing manner is a quiet belief in the value of connecting, a little at a time. Every small response gives her something to smile about—and something to celebrate with you.

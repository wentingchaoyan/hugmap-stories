MORNING · GETTING DRESSED

I can't pinch the button.

I want to do it, but my fingers won't move the way I need.
It makes me sad when people say I “can't.”

“It isn't that you can't.
Let's find a waythat makes it easier.”
RISU SENSEI'S POWERBreak movements into smaller parts and adjust tools and surroundings

ARRIVAL · STAIRS

I'm scared I might fall.

Everyone else climbs quickly,
but my legs are still getting ready.

“Instead of speed, start with
one step that feels safe. ”
USAGI SENSEI'S POWERSupport the body and build up small successes

ACTIVITY · GROUP INSTRUCTIONS

Words alone aren't enough for me.

What are we doing, and in what order?
Everyone starts, but I can't get moving.

“We can have the same goal
and a path you can see. ”
ZOU SENSEI'S POWERCreate another path to learning with pictures and concrete objects

LUNCH · EATING

Putting it in my mouth surprises me.

Texture, smell, size.
There is a reason behind “not eating.”

“Before practice,
make eating feel safe. ”
KABA SENSEI'S POWERFind a safe posture, form, and amount that make eating easier

PLAY · FRIENDS

I want to play together.

I look back and forth between the toy and my friend.
But no one has noticed my invitation yet.

“Your eyes, hands, and gestures—
they are all communication. ”
KOTORI SENSEI'S POWERNotice communication before it becomes words

GOING HOME · CHANGE OF PLANS

A sudden change is scary.

We can't take our usual route.
I stop, cry, and can't move.

“Behind behavior that looks difficult,
there is a person having difficulty. ”
FUKUROU SENSEI'S POWERConnect events, behavior, and the help that may be needed

HOME · SOMETHING IS DIFFERENT

Something feels hard today.

Even when I can't say “it hurts,”
my sleep and movements may be different.

“Reassurance matters,
and so does asking for advice. ”
KUMA SENSEI'S POWEROrganize what is different and connect the family with health advice

EVENING · TODAY'S NOTES

Don't lose what worked today.

What Mom noticed.
What worked with the teacher.
What I communicated.

“I'll keep the important things
safe in my pocket」
POCKET'S POWERKeep observations and records safe, then pass them to the next supporter

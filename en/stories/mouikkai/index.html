After the story

Let's discover the “words” in everyday gestures
together.

Reaching, turning away, pulling a sleeve, or looking back and forth between an object and a person: the same gesture can mean different things in different situations. Start by watching, waiting, and checking. HugMap helps you consider what your child may be communicating and how to respond by looking at expressions, movements, and what happens before and after.

  1. WatchWatch the eyes, hands, body, and what happens around the gesture
  2. WaitWait a little for the next signal
  3. CheckAsk the child, “Again?”

This story is based on general information. If you have concerns about hearing or development, consult a pediatrician, speech-language therapist, or another qualified professional.

After the story

Hand skills grow together through play.

Sensory play, putting in and taking out, stacking, using both hands, pinching, threading, scissors, and drawing are common connections in hand development. In real life these skills overlap, emerge in different orders, and every child has different strengths. Choose toys for the child's interests, current abilities, and safety—not age alone—and consult an occupational therapist when needed.
